Every route to loaded bases

Hits, walks, intentional walks, hit-by-pitches, errors, fielder's choices, interference, catcher's interference, an uncaught third strike, runner advances on wild pitches or passed balls, fielder's obstruction, placed runners in extras (ghost runner + walks or hits), and official corrections.

No keyword guessing: the official base occupancy decides. A tying play that leaves the bases loaded also qualifies. There is no upper inning limit — bottom 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, and beyond all count equally.

Tension rating explained

Each alert carries a tension score from 0 to 5, based on the combination of outs and the current count:

Outs: 0 outs (+1) · 1 out (+2) · 2 outs (+3)
Count: full count 3-2 (+2) · 2-strike or 3-ball (+1)

A bases-loaded alert at 2 outs with a 3-2 count is maximum tension (5) — one pitch can end the game.
