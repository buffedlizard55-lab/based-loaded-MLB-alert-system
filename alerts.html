One situation. Every game. This feed tracks only games that are tied in the bottom of the 9th inning or later, and narrates every step toward a walk-off bases-loaded situation — watch begins, runners reaching, the moment all three bases fill, tension rising, and the resolution (walk-off hit, third out, bases cleared, or extra innings). Nothing else triggers an alert.

A tied game whose home team is not batting is never dropped: it is shown as a held watch on the strip and in the counters, and the watch re-opens when the home half starts — so a delay, a scoreless ninth or a top half cannot lose the situation. The tabs above the feed only filter what you are reading; they never change what is polled or alerted.

Live data via the public MLB StatsAPI (statsapi.mlb.com). We read official runner occupancy on first, second and third — we do not guess from play text. Walks, hits, errors, catcher/fielder interference, automatic extra-inning runners, and official corrections are all caught by the same rule. Wild pitches, passed balls, steals, and balks move existing runners but don't add a new one; the chat reflects only what the official linescore actually says.
